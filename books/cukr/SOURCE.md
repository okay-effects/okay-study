# The CUKR residence card — official materials of the Office for Foreigners (UDSC), Poland

Fetched 2026-11-19 from the Lublin Voivodeship Office's page https://cudzoziemcy.lublin.uw.gov.pl/pl/cukr, which
republishes UDSC's documents (the footer of each: ul. Taborowa 33, Warszawa, www.gov.pl/udsc):

- `info-pl.pdf`, `info-ua.pdf` — "Informacja dotycząca procedury wydania karty pobytu z adnotacją «Poprzednio
  posiadacz ochrony czasowej» (tzw. karty pobytu CUKR)", 3 pages: who may get the card, the four conditions, how.
- `faq-pl.pdf`, `faq-ua.pdf` — "… pytania i odpowiedzi", 13–14 pages, sixty numbered questions with answers;
  questions 11–20 are CASES ("I had PESEL UKR for half a year, left for Ukraine, …").
- the `.txt` beside each: `pdftotext -layout`.

The official page: https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc/karta-pobytu-cukr. Official materials of a public office; kept
here as published, never edited. The law behind them changes: every answer made from these files must carry
this date and the source, and is information, not legal advice.
